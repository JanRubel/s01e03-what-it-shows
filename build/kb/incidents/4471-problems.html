The three problems

parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out with day and month swapped: “Our 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.” This is the bug #4471 describes. Fixed: fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales.

hint: the booking-v3 form tells people to type the month first. Its date field prints MM/DD/YYYY. People type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way, day first. The swap looks the same as parser, but the parser fix does not touch it: it only happens on the form channel, only for tenants on booking-v3 (Alder Physio, Copper Studio, Pale Harbour). Won’t fix: the booking-v3 form is retired, so no new report can come in this way.

day-shift: the date moves by one day. Nothing is swapped. “Invite says Tuesday 12 May. Calendar says Monday 11 May.” “Client in Lisbon got Tuesday. Client in Toronto got Monday.” One invite “arrived before I made the booking”. That is a timezone or day-boundary bug, on any tenant and any channel. The desk had already noted these reports as a one-day move (desk-notes.json) and filed them under parser anyway, because their tenant writes the day first.

Why they ended up in one ticket

  • Triage merges on the subject line alone (decision 0001). The three problems share the same subjects (“Month and day swapped?”, “Wrong day on the invite”), so subject matching cannot tell them apart. Both the triage page and decision 0001 are past the 180-day review mark.
  • Intake tags each report from the reporter’s browser. That is a guess: the cause depends on the tenant’s locale, booking form and channel (how the desk tells causes apart).
  • The export repeats some reports across its pages. They are counted once here.

What to do

  1. parser and hint are done: fixed, and retired.
  2. Give day-shift an owner. It is the one problem from #4471 nobody has fixed.
  3. When a report says “one day off”, read the body before merging: a flipped date and a moved date are different bugs.
